FARFALL

A shader-driven space game at planetary scale. Single-player sandbox: fly an orbit, land, ride the wormhole drive to the belt.

WASD translate · R/F up/down · arrows pitch/yaw · Q/E roll · Shift boost · H chaos drive · V warp stop · Space brake · M map · Esc menu · right mouse look

Needs WebGPU: Chrome or Edge 113+, Safari 26+, or the Meta Quest Browser. In a headset the ENTER VR button appears once the ship is up.
